2 moves to make

Each move is something at least two of the three competitors do and that fits the team and budget you gave. Either you are not doing it yet, or you do it at less than half the level they do.

Done by 3 of 3 competitors

1. Getting named in ChatGPT answers

The proof

  • Jotform: Named by ChatGPT in 3 of 3 buyer questions: "What is the best online form builder?"; "What are the best alternatives to Youform?"; "What is the best online form builder for small businesses?".
  • Typeform: Named by ChatGPT in 3 of 3 buyer questions: "What is the best online form builder?"; "What are the best alternatives to Youform?"; "What is the best online form builder for small businesses?".
  • Fillout: Named by ChatGPT in 3 of 3 buyer questions: "What is the best online form builder?"; "What are the best alternatives to Youform?"; "What is the best online form builder for small businesses?".
  • You: Named by ChatGPT in 0 of 3 buyer questions.

What it means. More buyers now ask ChatGPT for a shortlist before they ever visit Google. If you are not named, you are not on the shortlist.

Do this week. Ask ChatGPT the three questions below yourself. Look at the pages it cites, then pitch each one to include you, and publish a page that answers the question directly.

Done by 2 of 3 competitors

2. Free tools and templates that catch searches

The proof

  • Jotform: 52.4% of the search visits we sampled land on free tool and template pages. Top one: https://www.jotform.com/qr-code-generator/ (about 17,309 visits a month).
  • Typeform (does not do this): 5.1% of the search visits we sampled land on free tool and template pages. Top one: https://www.typeform.com/templates-sub-category/job-application (about 2,752 visits a month).
  • Fillout: 52.9% of the search visits we sampled land on free tool and template pages. Top one: https://www.fillout.com/ai-quiz-maker (about 1,131 visits a month).
  • You: 10.7% of the search visits we sampled land on free tool and template pages. Top one: https://youform.com/free-tools/google-form-qr-code/ (about 307 visits a month).

What it means. Buyers rarely search for your product. They search for a small job they need done today. A free page that does that job gets found, and some of those people need you next.

Do this week. List five small tasks your buyers search for right before they need you. Build the easiest one as a free page with no login.

Where this came from: DataForSEO ranked keywords, pulled 2026-11-25. Test: At least 15% of the search visits we sampled land on free tool or template pages.

Moves we left out

  • Public pages your users make, on your domain (done by 1 of 3). Only one of the three competitors does this, so it may be a one-off.
  • Comparison and alternative pages (done by 1 of 3). Only one of the three competitors does this, so it may be a one-off.
  • Guides that answer buyer questions (done by 1 of 3). Only one of the three competitors does this, so it may be a one-off.
  • Meta ads they keep running (done by 2 of 3). It needs about $1,000 a month to test ads, which is more than the team and budget you gave.

Why these three competitors

  • Jotform (jotform.com): named by ChatGPT in 3 of 3 answers, and ranks for 1,065 of the same Google searches.
  • Typeform (typeform.com): named by ChatGPT in 3 of 3 answers, and ranks for 887 of the same Google searches.
  • Fillout (fillout.com): named by ChatGPT in 3 of 3 answers, and ranks for 444 of the same Google searches.
